What about Google and “page strength”?

Acuity owns the .as.me address; you control a domain registered to you. An owned domain gives your content and future links a consistent destination, but Google does not automatically reward a domain just for being owned. Page views are not a transferable balance of ranking value. Moving indexed pages needs careful linking and, where possible, redirects; ranking gains are not guaranteed. The practical soft cost is dependency and a fragmented brand journey, not a dollar loss we can measure without your analytics.

Why the simpler versions matter.

The older small raster logo mixes fine lettering, glow and detailed feathers. Those details can blur at small sizes or fill in on stamps, embroidery and vinyl. Stretching its 220-pixel-wide reference cannot restore missing detail: at 300 pixels per inch, that is only about 0.73 inches wide.

The supplied full logo is still raster artwork; it has finite resolution. The new companion symbol is a true SVG vector and scales cleanly. Editable wordmark SVGs need fonts confirmed or outlined by a printer, and any print run needs a color proof. The kit is a prototype direction for your approval, including all AI-expanded imagery.
