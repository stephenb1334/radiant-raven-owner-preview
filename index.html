THE PERSON BEHIND YOUR PRACTICE

Meet Maria.
movement meets presence

Maria Tobin guides yoga, meditation, and sound-healing sessions at Radiant Raven. Her published classes make room for both an active practice and a slower, quieter one.

All-levels yoga brings together alignment, breath work, and guided meditation. Power yoga invites experienced students to build strength before settling into savasana. Yin yoga slows the pace with longer-held floor poses, while Yoga Nidra and sound sessions offer time for guided rest.
